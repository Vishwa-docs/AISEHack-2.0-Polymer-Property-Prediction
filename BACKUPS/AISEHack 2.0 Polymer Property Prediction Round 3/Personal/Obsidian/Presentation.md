Presentation (5-6 Minutes)
- **Scope:** Architecture, Novelty, and specific judge requests. Depth depends on judge interactions. Use the provided template.
- **Visuals:** Model Training charts, Scoring with Train/Test Splits, and architecture diagrams.
- **Interactive Element:** Create an interactive webpage to demonstrate results, model explainability, and generalization live.
- **Report Appendices:**
  - *Appendix A:* Experiments (Domains & Understandings) – Detail what failed and why.
  - *Appendix B:* Mathematical Maximum.
- **Research Angle:** Include a comparison with Qwen-type models.

**Presentation & Defense:**
- Clear documentation of domain-wise experiments and results.
- Identification of high-impact variables (e.g., 1% Tg accounting for 26% error).
- Models must be backed by solid reasoning and existing research papers.

- **Warning:** The panel possesses in-depth knowledge, will catch bluffs, and will grill heavily on architectural choices.
