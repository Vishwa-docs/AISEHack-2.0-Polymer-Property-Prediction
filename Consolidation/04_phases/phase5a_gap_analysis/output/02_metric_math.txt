========================================================================================
02 METRIC MATH - official metric = UNWEIGHTED MEAN of the seven per-target R2 values
   Score = (R2_tg + R2_egc + R2_egb + R2_ei + R2_eea + R2_nc + R2_eps) / 7
========================================================================================

KEY RESULT 1 - every target is worth EXACTLY 1/7 of the score, regardless of rows.
  +0.01 R2 on ANY single target = +0.00143 on the mean.  (+0.05 -> +0.00714)

KEY RESULT 2 - the marginal value of an absolute SSE reduction is (1/7)/TSS_j:
  cutting SSE by 1 unit on nc is worth ~4 MILLION times more than on tg,
  but the SSE you can realistically cut scales with TSS_j. R2 already normalises this.

KEY RESULT 3 - 'make one target perfect' ceilings (current mean 0.9024):
  perfect  tg -> mean 0.9172   (gain +0.0150)
  perfect egc -> mean 0.9150   (gain +0.0127)
  perfect egb -> mean 0.9127   (gain +0.0105)
  perfect  ei -> mean 0.9207   (gain +0.0184)
  perfect eea -> mean 0.9140   (gain +0.0117)
  perfect  nc -> mean 0.9153   (gain +0.0131)
  perfect eps -> mean 0.9188   (gain +0.0165)

  => Even a PERFECT Tg model gives mean 0.9175 only; perfect ei gives 0.9210.
     'Throw everything at Tg' alone CANNOT reach 0.935. (Refutes analysis #1's strategy.)

KEY RESULT 4 - TSS-weighted (pooled-within-target) metric would give tg ~98-99% weight:
   tg: test-proxy TSS   32,865,685  = 99.99% of total
  egc: test-proxy TSS        3,322  =  0.01% of total
  egb: test-proxy TSS          873  =  0.00% of total
   ei: test-proxy TSS          161  =  0.00% of total
  eea: test-proxy TSS          179  =  0.00% of total
   nc: test-proxy TSS            8  =  0.00% of total
  eps: test-proxy TSS          182  =  0.00% of total
  Under such a metric, LB ~ R2_tg = 0.8953 and the 0.891 private LB coincidence would be explained.
  The official page says 'mean ... across the seven targets' = unweighted. Empirical check in script 03.

KEY RESULT 5 - what it takes to reach 0.935 (need +0.0326 mean = +0.228 R2 spread over 7 targets):
  Per-target R2 needed if every target improves by the same amount: +0.0326 each.
  That means ~0.928 avg (tg 0.928, ei 0.904, eps 0.917, ...) - i.e. state-of-the-art on EVERY target.
